# Quant – Quantitative Finance & AI

A comprehensive collection of implementations, simulations, algorithms, and AI-powered applications focused on **Quantitative Finance, Financial Mathematics, Algorithmic Trading, Portfolio Optimization, Financial Machine Learning, and AI Agents**.

This repository is dedicated to learning quantitative finance through practical implementation, starting with fundamental statistical concepts and progressing towards advanced mathematical models, trading systems, and autonomous financial research agents.

The goal is to build a strong foundation in quantitative research while exploring how Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models can transform financial analysis and investment research.
